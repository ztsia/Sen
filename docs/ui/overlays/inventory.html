What's wrong with the sheet itself

  1. Three layers of chrome before any content: a 100 px grab bar, a title row with a ✕, and a rule under it. Every sheet spends its first 90 px on them.
  2. No shared inside. Choices appear as edge-to-edge text rows, icon rows with chevrons, stacked buttons and chips, depending on who built the sheet. A chevron here means opens a screen elsewhere.
  3. Categories lose their tiles. The picker is 17 lines of plain text, while Add expense shows the same categories as a grid of coloured tiles.
  4. Sheets for one or two choices. Mark this as… can hold a single row. Who paid? holds two.
  5. Chains. Payment's ⋯ menu opens a sheet that opens another step: three surfaces for one action.
  6. Long forms squeezed in. Four sheets hit the 80% cap and scroll, with the browser's own mm/dd/yyyy date box inside.
