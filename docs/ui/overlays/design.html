Sen’s overlays, three options

Three ways to build Sen’s bottom sheet, each drawn with the same four sheets at true size: pick the one you’d rather hold, by eye. The menu and the dialog further down go with any of them.

At a glanceThe same two sheets in each option: a form with the keyboard up, where they differ most, and the category picker.

A · QuietOn the bottom edge, top corners rounded. A small handle, the title, a quiet ✕ and no rule: content starts under the title. The main button is pinned to the bottom of the sheet, above the keyboard.

Copper · the same sheet on another look’s tokens: Pick in light, Form in dark · swipe sideways

Good at: the least chrome, so the content is the sheet. The one filled button sits under the thumb and stays put, and it looks the same in all six looks.

Costs: the pinned button takes 64 px from a form while the keyboard is up. ✕ and Archive are neighbours at the top, out of the thumb’s reach. In dark only the scrim and a hairline part it from the screen.

Motion: rises from the bottom edge in 200 ms and falls the same way. On a step the top edge eases to the new height while the button holds the bottom; content scrolling under the button fades.

B · Floating cardInset 8 px from the sides and the bottom, all four corners rounded from the look’s card radius, lifted on a shadow. No handle: the whole header drags. ✕ sits in a filled circle, and the main button is inside the card at its bottom.

Copper · the same sheet on another look’s tokens: Pick in light, Form in dark · swipe sideways

Good at: reading as one object, with the clearest ✕ of the three. Both of a form’s actions sit at the thumb, and each look shows in its corners (18 px in Mercury, 12 in Copper).

Costs: 16 px of width and of height. No handle says it can be swiped down. The screen and the tab bar show around it, and with the keyboard up a strip shows between. In dark the shadow is lost. Archive beside Save can be hit by mistake.

Motion: rises and settles clear of the edges in 200 ms, and falls back. On a step it grows upward from its bottom edge. A drag anywhere on the header moves the whole card.

C · Action barOn the bottom edge, with a handle and one bar: Cancel on the left, the title centred, the main action on the right. Nothing is hidden by the keyboard and there’s no bottom button. A Pick or Read sheet has only the title and ✕ in the bar.

Copper · the same sheet on another look’s tokens: Pick in light, Form in dark · swipe sideways

Good at: the keyboard: Cancel and Save are always in the same two places and can’t be covered. The destructive action has the bottom of the form to itself, in full words.

Costs: Save is about 590 px up from the bottom edge here, a stretch for one thumb, so the keyboard’s Done key must save too. The title shares its row and must stay under about 22 characters. The tinted bar is a layer the others don’t have, and in a Pick or Read sheet it holds only ✕.

Motion: rises and falls like A. The bar stays still while the content under it eases to the new height, and Cancel cross-fades to Back on a step.

Menus and the dialogThe same whichever sheet you choose (D155). A menu opens beside what was tapped and holds two to five actions; a dialog is only for something that can’t be undone.

Recommendation

I’d take A · Quiet: it removes the most chrome, keeps the one filled button under the thumb whether or not the keyboard is up, and looks the same in all six looks because it asks nothing of them but a colour and a radius. Its weak spot is Archive beside ✕, and if that bothers you, C’s answer (the last row of the form) drops into A without changing anything else.

Every phone is 390 × 844, scaled to fit the page. Inside a sheet every colour, radius and font is the look’s token; the scrim, the shadow and the keyboard stand-in are plain black and grey, since a look has no token for them. Names and amounts are the made-up month the app’s own screenshots use. Source: docs/ui/overlays/design-src/.
