The details are in ui/patterns.md; these shape the map.

  • The tab bar shows on the five tab roots and on screens pushed from them. It hides during a task with an end: first-run, payday, confirm, manual, balance-check and scan.

  • Android's back gesture closes the top sheet first, then goes back one screen (uiux).

  • A person sees only what they can use (D52). Without capture there's no accounts, balance-check, gap or capture setting. With a feature switched off, its screens, menu entries and cards aren't there. With the agent off, the tab bar has four slots.

  • Health warnings (§18) sit at the top of home until fixed, each with a button that fixes it.

  • Rows still in the outbox show beside synced ones, marked Not synced yet (§5).
  • Every change happens at once, with Undo in a toast (§6.7). Only a destructive action that can't be undone asks first.

Home

homeWhat's left until payday, and does anything need me?

Shows (D59), top to bottom:

  1. Header: the Sen wordmark, and ≡ for the menu.

  2. Health warnings, if any (§18): Capture is off, Daily jobs have stopped, Backups are failing. Each has its fix button.

  3. The Payday card, on payday (D60), until the flow is done or dismissed. After it, the card shrinks to the payday plan's progress (2 of 5 moves done) until every move is ticked or you hide it.

  4. Left until payday, large: income received this cycle minus spending this cycle (D14), with 12 days to go from the expected payday. Below zero it reads Over by RM… in the warning colour.

  5. Pace: Spent RM2,115 this cycle and RM140 less than last cycle by this day.

  6. Needs attention: its count, then its first three items, each with its buttons, then See all. Hidden when there's nothing.

  7. A quiet row: total balance, and the gap the last balance check found, with its date.

Does: tap the large figure for cycle; pace for insights; an item's buttons answer it there; See all for attention; the balance row for accounts.

From: the Home tab; opening the app; a widget; most notifications.

Varies:

  • Without capture (§9.6): no balance row and no gap. Left until payday appears once the person enters this cycle's income; until then the large figure is spending, with its pace.

  • Before the first salary: the cycle runs from the opening date (D14), with no income in it, so the large figure reads Spent since you started instead of a misleading negative.

  • The first cycle has no last cycle: the pace line says so.

  • Offline: the figures include outbox rows (§5), and a small chip says how many aren't synced.

confirmDid Sen read this receipt right, and what part was mine?

Three steps (D63), each one screen, with the photo one tap away:

  1. Check: the photo on top (pinch to zoom), then merchant, date and time, service charge, tax and total. Doubtful fields are marked, and the sum is checked against the total.

  2. Items: tick what's yours (all of them to start with); each item's category, from the reader's suggestion; your share, updating as you tick.

  3. Who paid: Me, a person, or Someone new; pax for a meal; Split with friends; and, quieter, Not a payment: keep as evidence (for a claim, §6.4).

After Done: a toast says what happened (§6.4):

  • Attached to RM58.30 on Ryt, with Undo
  • several payments match: a sheet asks which

  • no payment yet: Waiting for its payment; it attaches itself (with capture), or the payment is created (without capture)

  • someone else paid: You owe Mei RM24.40 (D20)

Leaving early keeps the receipt in Needs attention, read and waiting.

Journeys 34

Each journey: what starts it, the steps, what happens when it goes wrong, and how it ends.

Setting up

First run first-runStarts: the owner opens the shell for the first time, installed from a GitHub Actions build (§3).
  1. sign-in: email, then the 6-digit code from the email.

  2. Notification access. Open settings goes to Android's notification-access page; switch Sen on and come back. Capture starts now: anything it sees waits in the outbox (§9.5).

  3. Xiaomi settings: Autostart on, battery saver No restrictions, and the app locked in recents. Each step opens the right page; locking in recents is a gesture, so that step shows how.

  4. Accounts: Public Bank, Ryt, TNG eWallet, GO+ and GrabPay Wallet, each with the balance its app shows right now (§7).

  5. Your name as the banks print it, such as TAN WEI MING, for spotting transfers between your own accounts (D17). More than one is allowed.

  6. Expected payday: the last working day of the month (the default), or a day number (D14).

  7. home. The large figure reads Spent since you started until the first salary lands.

When it goes wrong

  • The switch is greyed out (Android 16's restricted settings, §6.2): Sen explains, then opens its own app-info page, where ⋮ → Allow restricted settings unlocks it; then back to step 2.

  • Notification access refused: Skip for now is allowed. home then shows Capture is off with Fix until it's granted.

  • The app is closed mid-setup: it reopens at the same step.

  • Wrong or expired code: Send a new code. Offline: Signing in needs a connection.
  • Anything that breaks later shows on home as a warning with a fix; the steps never come back (D26).

Done: home, with capture on.

An invited friend's first day invitedStarts: an invitation email (D52).
  1. The link opens sign-in in the browser, the email filled in; then the code.

  2. The short first run (§9.6): expected payday, then the starting categories, where any can be switched off.

  3. home: Needs attention and spending, no balances. In a browser, Sen offers Add to Home screen (the PWA).

  4. Their first receipt (scan-after) creates its payment, since no notification is coming (§6.4).

When it goes wrong: an expired invitation says Ask for a new invitation; an email that wasn't invited gets Sen is invitation-only.

Everyday capture

Paying at a known merchant pay-knownStarts: a payment at a merchant Sen has a rule for, such as KOPI KAWAN.

  1. Ryt notifies. In native code, the listener stores the raw text, a template reads it, and the merchant rule sets the category. The payment is done, with no review (D24).

  2. If the quiet scan prompt is on (§6.3): RM8.90 · KOPI KAWAN · Drinks & desserts, with one button, Scan receipt. It clears itself after 10 minutes.

  3. The outbox syncs when there's a network. home and the widgets reflect it at their next refresh.

When it goes wrong

  • Offline: nothing changes for you; sync waits for the network (§6.2).

  • The same notification again (the listener reconnects, a reinstall): the dedupe key makes it a no-op (§6.5).

  • A Grab charge that names no restaurant waits up to 10 minutes for its context notification (§6.2).

  • A card hold at a petrol pump is ignored; the final charge is the payment (§6.3).

Done: a categorised payment, with no tap.

Paying at a new merchant pay-newStarts: a payment at a merchant Sen hasn't seen, such as NASI KANDAR ABC.

  1. The category prompt (§6.3): RM11.00 · NASI KANDAR ABC, with the two best guesses and Scan receipt.

  2. Tap a category: the payment takes it, and a merchant rule is made, so this merchant never asks again.

  3. Or tap Scan receipt: scan-after. The receipt answers the question: the payment takes its items' categories, and the merchant's rule takes the receipt's largest category.

  4. Or tap the notification itself: Sen opens at its row in attention, with every category.

When it goes wrong

  • Swiped away or ignored: it waits in Needs attention (D22).

  • Another new merchant before you answer: the new prompt replaces the old one in the shade; the old one waits in Needs attention (D24).

  • The wrong button: fix it in txn (fix).

  • Offline: the buttons work; the answer syncs later.

Done: a categorised payment and a new merchant rule.

Paying a friend pay-personStarts: money sent to a person, such as RM20.00 to ALI BIN ABU BAKAR from TNG.

  1. The person prompt (§6.3, D20): your last answer for Ali first, then Lent and Other…. When you owe Ali an amount, Paying back: Dinner, 3 Oct comes first, even offline (§5).

  2. Tap one. There's never a silent rule for a person (D20).

  3. A name Sen hasn't seen that doesn't match a known one asks Who is this? once, in attention (D61). Later payments to that name know who it is.

When it goes wrong

  • Paying back a different amount from what you owe: within RM5 it still matches (D20); otherwise Needs attention asks which bill, if any.

  • Your own name is a transfer, not a payment (own-transfer).

Done: the payment has its what, and the person is known.

Money in from someone money-inStarts: money arrives from a name that isn't yours, such as RM40.00 from ALI BIN ABU BAKA.
  1. No notification asks; it waits in attention (§6.6).

  2. One open amount Ali owes you, and it fits: Sen matches it as the repayment, ticks the share on any split, and the row clears itself.

  3. Otherwise the row asks: Repaid (which bill, when there are several), Income (such as a gift), or Transfer.

  4. A name Sen hasn't seen asks Who is this? first (D61).

When it goes wrong: a payment that's more than what's owed links what's owed, and the row asks about the rest.

Done: the money is a repayment, income or a transfer.

Moving money between your own accounts own-transferStarts: salary lands in Public Bank, and you send RM2,000 on to Ryt.

  1. Ryt notifies You've received RM2,000.00 from TAN WEI MING. Your own name makes it a transfer, never income (D17).

  2. Public Bank doesn't notify money leaving it (§21 Q16), so one side is missing. The first time, attention asks Where did it come from?, with your accounts and Elsewhere.

  3. Your answer becomes a transfer rule. Next time, the missing side is filled in by itself and marked Filled in in activity (§7).

  4. If the real side ever arrives, it replaces the filled-in one.

Moves inside one app (eWallet → GO+) and GrabPay top-ups from the Public Bank card are transfers by their templates (§6.2, D38).

Done: one transfer, both accounts right, no spending.

A refund refundStarts: money in from a merchant.
  1. It waits in attention, with Refund of… as well as the money-in buttons.
  2. Refund of… lists recent payments to that merchant; pick the purchase.
  3. The refund takes its amount off that purchase's spending, in the purchase's cycle (D21).

Done: the purchase's spending is lower; the refund is never income.

Receipts

Scanning a receipt after paying scan-afterStarts: the quiet scan prompt, the category prompt's third button, a widget, the launcher shortcut, or plus.

  1. scan: ML Kit finds the edges and crops; adjust the crop while the receipt is in your hand, then save. In a browser: the camera, then crop.

  2. The image is resized and uploaded (§6.4); reading.

  3. confirm step 1, Check: compare the marked fields with the photo; fix any.
  4. Step 2, Items: untick what isn't yours.
  5. Step 3, Who paid: Me; pax 3 for a meal; Done.
  6. Matching finds the RM58.30 payment within 3 hours (§6.4) and attaches the receipt: items, your share, evidence. The toast says Attached to RM58.30 on Ryt, with Undo.

When it goes wrong

  • Some items weren't yours: your share is what you ticked, with tax and service pro rata; the rest is owed to you (D19), and Split with friends is offered.

  • Several payments match: a sheet asks which.

  • No payment yet: receipt-first.
  • Reading fails: Couldn't read it; manual with the image beside it. The image is never lost.
  • Offline: Saved. It uploads when you're back online; it waits in Needs attention.
  • You leave half-way: it waits in Needs attention, read and waiting.
  • The same file twice: Already added, and it opens the first one (§6.5).
  • The total differs from the payment (a tip, rounding): they don't match by themselves. Attach to a payment… attaches items and evidence only, and never changes the amount, your share or the category (§6.4).

Done: the payment carries its receipt, items and your share.

A receipt before its payment receipt-firstStarts: a receipt confirmed with no payment to match: scanned before Ryt's notification arrived, or a phone bill PDF forwarded days after it was paid.
  1. The toast says Waiting for its payment; it attaches itself. The receipt waits in attention.
  2. The payment's notification arrives: matching attaches the receipt, and the row clears itself.
  3. It never comes, so the row offers:

  • A new employer's first salary: mark it as salary once; a merchant rule learns the new name.
  • The agent off, or at its cap: steps 3 and 4 aren't there.
