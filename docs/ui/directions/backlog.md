# Looks in the backlog

The pool holds six looks, and four play each year (D78). More can join later. These two ideas were
offered alongside Mercury and Copper on 6 Oct and kept for later rather than dropped. Neither is
designed yet. A session that picks one up builds it to Minted's and Instrument's level: a crafted
material with a money tie, a signature large figure, a generated avatar with real detail, and
details only that world has (the `S2` notes on it are in the owner's private design archive).

## Clay

The first writing was bookkeeping: tallies pressed into clay in Mesopotamia, 5,000 years ago.

- **Material:** terracotta and stoneware, with marks pressed into it. Light is raw clay, a pale slip and
  iron-oxide ink. Dark is fired stoneware, near black, with a warm glaze.
- **Large figure:** pressed into the clay, lit low from one side.
- **Sen:** a clay disc. A stylus presses a ring of marks while it works; its helpers are small tablets.
- **Cycle strip:** tally marks, one pressed each day.
- **Payday:** a cylinder seal, used then to sign and authorise, rolls across Home to sign the new cycle.
- **Details:** seal impressions, glaze pooling in the marks, kiln marks.
- **To weigh:** terracotta sits close to the warning orange, so warnings must lean on their icon and
  words. The pressed marks look like a museum piece unless they're kept spare.

## Salt

Salary comes from the Latin *salarium*, from *sal*, salt, so payday ties straight in.

- **Material:** sea-salt crystals. Light is salt white and warm grey, with a hint of rose salt. Dark is
  slate, with the crystals catching the light.
- **Large figure:** crystallises along its strokes.
- **Sen:** a hopper crystal, a stepped hollow cube, that grows step by step while it works, with seed
  crystals for its helpers.
- **Cycle strip:** a salt pan, crystals forming day by day.
- **Payday:** crystals bloom across the figure.
- **To weigh:** white crystals on pale screens are low in contrast. The salt-pay story is partly
  legend. Its pale palette sits nearest Instrument's.
